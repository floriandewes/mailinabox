{% trans %}Greylisting{% endtrans %}

{% trans %}Greylisting is off by default, so incoming mail is accepted on the first connection. An administrator can turn it on to cut down on spam. When greylisting is on, your box initially rejects mail from people you haven’t received mail from before. Legitimate mail servers attempt redelivery shortly afterwards, but most spam does not. Mail from someone new, such as a confirmation while you are registering on a website, can then be delayed by at least 3 minutes, depending on when the remote server retries.{% endtrans %}
